# Editorial notes — original six-story edition, 20 November 2026

The current fifteen-story revision is documented in `stories.json`. The notes
below describe the earlier submitted artwork and are retained as development
history, not as a claim that the current image still has six panels.

## Scope and verification

- [evidence] Target: six developments that occurred or were reported on 20 November 2026, covering Europe, the Middle East, Asia, US/global markets, climate diplomacy and humanitarian assistance.
- [evidence] Each selected claim was checked in two editorially independent newsrooms. A republication is credited to its originating wire service, not counted as a second independent source. The hunger item additionally has the primary WFP announcement.
- [evidence] The articles below were retrieved and read on 20 November 2026 after the US stock-market close. All selected articles identify the target date. Archived text-only retrieval evidence is outside the entry repository under workspace/research/news-image-20261120-build/.
- [assumption] Independent editorial coverage is not independent eyewitness verification of every official statement. Attribution is retained where reporting rests on officials or a conflict party. This is a selected digest, not an exhaustive census of the day's news.

## 1. Ukraine — bridges and power infrastructure hit

- [evidence] Reuters and AP report Russian missile/drone attacks on 20 November, damaged Kyiv bridges and energy infrastructure, and at least eight deaths across Ukraine according to Ukrainian officials. Eight is the countrywide toll, not the number killed on a bridge or in Kyiv alone.
- Final image wording: “Kyiv bridges hit” / “Officials: Russian strikes kill 8 across Ukraine.”
- Reuters, Lina Golovnya and Valentyn Ogirenko, 20 November:
  https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/world/europe/fire-broke-out-ukraines-kyiv-after-missile-strike-mayor-says-2026-11-20/
- Associated Press, republished by UNB, 20 November:
  https://www.unb.com.bd/category/World/russian-strikes-kill-8-in-ukraine-as-moscow-vows-retaliation-for-bridge-attack/200850
- [evidence] Both reports rely in part on Ukrainian officials. Do not depict casualty locations, contested targeting intent, or specific destruction beyond what the reports establish.

## Visual rights and editorial constraints

- [evidence] All pictorial assets are authored geometric drawings in tools/make_image.py. No news photographs, logos, downloaded artwork, competitor images, maps or font files are included.
- [assumption] System-installed typefaces may be rasterized for labels; the font programs themselves are not distributed. The renderer records the chosen font filenames in its local build report.
- [from docs] The image must clearly name the day, favor consequential and geographically/topically varied events, preserve attribution/uncertainty, and remain legible near 300 pixels wide. More words or decorative detail alone are not goals.
- [evidence] Editorial scope is limited to these six verified stories. A bridge, port, negotiation table, agreement documents, food bowl and falling-index plot communicate distinct subjects without pretending to be documentary scenes.
